# Agmeta Client Service Agreement

## How to use this template

This is one master agreement for every Agmeta project: AI agents, WhatsApp bots, websites and apps. The project-specific details (features, price, dates) go in **Annex A: Scope of Work**, so the main terms never change between clients.

- Fill every `[BRACKETED]` field before sending.
- The client accepts in the client portal (name + OTP), which records the time, IP address and document version. For projects above ₹1,00,000, also get an Aadhaar eSign through a provider such as Zoho Sign, Leegality or Digio, which carries stronger legal standing under the IT Act.
- **This is a template, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer in your state review it once, and check with your CA whether stamp duty applies to e-signed agreements in your state.

## 2. Scope, timeline and your responsibilities

**2.1 Scope.** We will deliver only what is listed in Annex A. Anything not written in Annex A is outside the scope and is handled as a Change Request (clause 4.3).

**2.2 Not included unless listed in Annex A:** content writing, photography, logo or brand design, data entry beyond the stated limit, third-party fees (clause 6.3), app store approval (which Apple and Google control), SEO rankings, and guaranteed sales, leads or call volumes.

**2.3 Timeline.** The delivery dates in Annex A start from the later of (a) the day the advance is received and (b) the day we receive everything listed in clause 2.4.

**2.4 What you provide.** Within `[7]` days of signing, you will give us the content, logos, images, product and price lists, FAQs, logins and approvals listed in Annex A. You confirm you have the rights to everything you give us.

**2.5 One point of contact.** You will name one person who gives feedback and approvals. We act on that person's written instructions (WhatsApp or email counts as written).

**2.6 Communication.** We share progress updates weekly. Support and feedback go through the client portal or the project WhatsApp, on working days, `[10 AM–7 PM IST]`.

**2.7 Content you approve.** We may prepare text, FAQs, images and your agent's knowledge base using AI tools, from information you give or link to us. You review and approve this content in the portal before it goes live, and you are responsible for the accuracy of facts, prices, offers and any health, legal or financial claims in it. Images we add are your own, licensed, or AI-generated.

**2.8 Team and subcontractors.** We may use vetted team members or freelancers for parts of the work. Each of them is bound by confidentiality and assigns their work to us, sees only what their task needs, and does not contact you directly. Agmeta stays fully responsible for the work and remains your single point of contact.

**3.5 Late payment.** If an invoice is unpaid 7 days after its due date, we may pause work and hosted services (clause 5.3) until it is paid. Unpaid amounts carry interest at `[1.5]`% per month, or, where Agmeta is a registered micro or small enterprise, the interest set by the MSMED Act, 2006, whichever is higher.

**3.6 Monthly fees.** Monthly fees (AI agent retainers, care plans) are billed in advance on the `[1st]` of each month through an automatic payment link or mandate, and can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice.

**3.7 The advance is non-refundable** once work has started, because it covers discovery, planning and work already done (see clause 5).

## 4. Revisions, change requests and acceptance

**4.1 Two revision rounds are included.** After we share the working project (a staging link, test number or demo build), you get **two rounds of revisions** at no extra cost.

**4.2 What a revision round is.** One round is **one consolidated list** of changes, sent through the client portal within `[7]` days of the delivery it relates to. We complete each round within `[5]` working days. Changes sent one at a time on different days count as separate rounds. A revision changes something already in Annex A: text, colours, layout, images, the agent's answers or tone, or fixes to things that don't work as described.

**4.3 Change Requests.** Anything that is not a revision is a Change Request: new pages, screens, features or integrations, a change in direction after design approval, or any change after the two rounds are used. For each Change Request we send a written quote with price and time. Work on it starts only after you approve the quote in the portal and pay it. Extra hours are charged at `[₹1,000]` per hour unless quoted otherwise. Change Requests may move the delivery date.

**4.4 Acceptance.** When the revisions are done, you approve the project in the portal. If you do not approve or send a revision list within `[7]` days of a delivery, the delivery is treated as **accepted**, and the next invoice becomes due.

**4.5 Using it means accepting it.** If you start using the project with your customers (a live website, app or agent), it is treated as accepted.

## 5. Cancellation, pauses and delays

**5.1 If you cancel.** You may cancel by written notice at any time. You then pay for all work done up to that date, and at least the payment of the milestone currently in progress. Amounts already paid are not refunded. We hand over the work completed so far once all amounts due are paid.

**5.2 If you go silent.** If we cannot get the content, feedback or approval we need from you for `[14]` days, we pause the project and tell you in writing. If the pause lasts `[30]` days, we may treat the project as complete as it stands: the remaining milestone payments become due, and restarting later carries a restart fee of `[10]`% of the project fee and a new timeline.

**5.3 If you don't pay.** If any amount is overdue by more than 7 days, we may pause work. For services we host or run on our accounts (AI agents, bots, staging sites), we may suspend them after giving you `[7]` days' written notice, and restore them once payment is received.

**5.4 If we cancel.** We may end this Agreement if you breach it and do not fix the breach within 14 days of written notice. If we end it for our own reasons, we refund any amount paid for work not yet done.

**5.5 What carries on.** Clauses on payment owed, ownership, confidentiality, liability and disputes continue after this Agreement ends.

## 6. Ownership, handover and third-party costs

**6.1 Ownership passes on full payment.** When every amount under this Agreement is paid, you own the final deliverables made specifically for you: designs, content we wrote for you, the source code of your website or app, and your agent's prompts and knowledge base. Until then, Agmeta owns them and you may only review them on staging.

**6.2 What stays ours.** Our reusable tools, templates, code libraries, agent frameworks and know-how remain Agmeta's. You get a permanent, non-exclusive licence to use them as part of your project. Open-source and third-party components stay under their own licences.

**6.3 Third-party accounts and costs.** Accounts are opened in **your name** wherever possible, and you pay their fees directly or reimburse us at cost. These include: domain, hosting, WhatsApp Business API messages, phone numbers and call minutes, AI model usage above the plan limit, payment gateway fees, email services, Google Play and Apple developer accounts, and paid plugins or themes. Usage included in a monthly plan is listed in Annex A.

**6.4 Handover.** After final payment, within `[3]` working days we deliver the handover kit listed in Annex B: access to all accounts, source code (repository transfer or download), credentials through a secure one-time link, documentation, a recorded walkthrough, and the final invoice. You confirm receipt in the portal.

**6.5 Portfolio.** We may show the finished project (name, screenshots, a short description) in our portfolio and social media, unless you ask us in writing not to. We never share your customers' data.

## 7. Warranty, support and AI services

**7.1 Free bug-fix warranty.** For `[30]` days after handover, we fix free of charge anything that does not work as described in Annex A. This does not cover new features, changes made by you or another developer, third-party outages, or changes by Apple, Google, Meta or other platforms.

**7.2 Care plan (optional).** After the warranty, support and updates are available on a monthly care plan, priced in Annex A. Without a care plan, support is charged at the hourly rate in clause 4.3.

**7.3 AI agents and bots.** You understand that:

- AI agents generate answers automatically and can sometimes be wrong. We test each agent before launch (at least `[50]` sample questions) and set it to hand over to your team when unsure.
- The agent will not give medical, legal or financial advice, and will only quote prices and facts you have approved. You are responsible for keeping that information up to date.
- The agent tells callers and chatters that it is an AI assistant.
- You will collect any consent your customers must give before being messaged, called or recorded, and use WhatsApp only with customers who opted in.

**7.4 Platforms.** WhatsApp, voice, AI model and app store providers can change their rules, prices or approvals. We will adapt the project where possible; work needed because of such changes after the warranty is a Change Request.

## 8. Confidentiality, data, liability and disputes

**8.1 Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's business information private and uses it only for this project, during and after the Agreement.

**8.2 Personal data.** Where we handle your customers' personal data, we act on your instructions, collect only what the project needs, keep it secure, and delete or return it when the project or care plan ends. Both parties will follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

**8.3 Limit of liability.** Our total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the `[6]` months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect losses such as lost profit, lost business or lost data, except for breach of confidentiality or wilful misconduct.

**8.4 Events outside control.** Neither party is responsible for delays caused by events outside its reasonable control, such as platform outages, natural disasters or government action.

**8.5 Disputes.** We will first try to settle any dispute by talking for 15 days. If that fails, it goes to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in `[CITY]`, in English. Courts in `[CITY]` have jurisdiction. Indian law applies.

**8.6 Whole agreement.** This Agreement, Annex A and approved Change Requests are the complete agreement. Changes must be in writing and accepted by both parties (portal acceptance counts). Electronic acceptance through the client portal is valid as a signature.
